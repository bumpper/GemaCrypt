 
1:1	Paul, an apostle not from men nor through man, but through Jesus Christ, to the brethren who are of Laodicea. 
1:2	Grace to you and peace from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 
1:3	I give thanks to Christ through all my prayers that you are continuing in him and persevering in his works, looking forward to the promise [of salvation] in the Day of Judgment. 
1:4	Neither does the vain prattling of some intruders beguile you, that they may divert you from the truth of the Gospel which is preached by me. 
1:5	And now God will cause that those who are [disciples] of mine will continue serving, to the increase of the truth of the Gospel, and performing goodness and the work of salvation of eternal life. 
1:6	And now well-known are my bonds which I suffer in Christ, in which I rejoice and am glad. 
1:7	And this to me is for everlasting salvation, which also is wrought by your prayers, and by the superintendence of the Holy Spirit, whether through life or through death. 
1:8	For to me, to live is to be in Christ, and to die is joy. 
1:9	And likewise he will work his mercy in you that you may have the same love, and may be of one mind. 
1:10	Therefore, dearly beloved, as you have heard in my presence, so hold fast and work in the fear of God, and it will be life eternal for you. 
1:11	For it is God who works in you. 
1:12	And do without retreating whatever you do. 
1:13	And for the rest, dearly beloved, rejoice in Christ, and beware of those who are sordid in worldly gain. 
1:14	Let all your petitions be made openly before God, and be firm in the thinking of Christ. 
1:15	And do those things that are sound, and true, and sober, and just, and amiable. 
1:16	And what you have heard and received, retain in your heart. 
1:17	And peace shall be with you. 
1:18	The saints salute you. 
1:19	The grace of the Lord Jesus be with your spirit. 
1:20	And cause [this letter] to be read to the Colossians and that of the Colossians to you.
